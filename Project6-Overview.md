# Help End Homelessness – ArcGIS Pro Case Study


This project applied advanced spatial analysis in ArcGIS Pro to model homelessness risk and resource allocation in Los Angeles County. The case study demonstrated how GIS can support equitable decision-making by combining demographic, economic, and housing variables to identify areas most in need of intervention.


The goal was to gain experience using GIS to address a real-world social issue by building a risk index, mapping vulnerable populations, and developing prevention strategies. This work emphasizes the role of GIS in shaping data-driven policies around housing insecurity and community equity.

## Skills Gained

- Constructed a composite risk index using census tract-level variables (poverty, unemployment, housing, and health).

- Conducted hotspot and suitability analyses to prioritize prevention areas.

- Mapped and characterized homeless communities to identify population-specific risk factors.

- Designed planning scenarios to support resource allocation and stakeholder consensus.

## Tools & Data

- ArcGIS Pro (spatial statistics, suitability modeling, map design)

- U.S. Census data, Los Angeles County socioeconomic indicators

## Key Insights
Spatial analysis revealed that homelessness risk is highly concentrated in specific tracts, with prevention programs most impactful when targeted in areas combining high poverty and limited housing access. The project underscored how GIS can translate complex data into actionable strategies for public policy and social equity.
