The histogram of the distribution of percentage of foreign born in Seattle has a shape that is
right skewed. The peak of the data between 11% and 14% is the most occurring percentage
of foreign born in a census tract. The areas with the highest percent of the population being
foreign include Rainer Valley, Chinatown, and South Seattle. Census tracts in South Seattle
is where most of the higher percentages of foreign-born populations are. Census tract 9100
near Chinatown had the highest percentage of 54% of the population be foreign born.
Census tract 7001 had the lowest percentage of foreign-born population with only 2.6%. As
seen in figure 1, majority of the darkest census tracts are in South Seattle. My first figure
used natural breaks classification while my second figure used quantile classification.
Since my second map used quantile classification, the number of data values were made
to be equal across classes. This made it look like there were more census tracts with high
percentages of foreign born than in map 1. The first map using natural breaks is more
accurate since it breaks up the data based on groupings of similar data.
