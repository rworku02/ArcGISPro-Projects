GIS should be used to study where communities are being hurt by law enforcement practices since this leads to more bad than good in the long run if there isn’t racial equity.

Their study found that Black men were frequently stopped more by police in countries like Brazil or England (Carvalho et al., 2022). Police were questioning Black people at much higher rates and brings up the issue of racial bias in law enforcement, and how much of a systemic issue this is if police aren’t practicing just actions.
