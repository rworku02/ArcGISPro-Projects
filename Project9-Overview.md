This project focused on communicating earthquake risk in California using ArcGIS Online and StoryMaps. The workflow combined web maps, interactive dashboards, and 3D web scenes to illustrate the frequency and severity of earthquakes, population exposure near fault lines, and preparedness strategies. By integrating multimedia and GIS applications, the project created a public-facing story that effectively conveys geospatial risk information to a broad audience.


The project aimed to develop practical skills in public-facing GIS visualization and storytelling. By linking interactive dashboards and 3D scenes with narrative content, I gained experience in creating engaging, data-driven story maps that translate complex spatial data into actionable insights for public awareness and disaster preparedness.

## Skills Gained

- Configuring ArcGIS Online dashboards to display real-time earthquake data with indicators, maps, and legends

- Filtering and summarizing point data dynamically to reflect user-selected map extents

- Building interactive 3D web scenes to visualize population density and proximity to earthquake faults

- Creating slides/bookmarks in 3D scenes for major population centers

- Developing 3D web applications to enable intuitive navigation and exploration of spatial data

- Integrating dashboards and 3D apps into ArcGIS StoryMaps with additional multimedia content

- Applying design principles and dark-theme color schemes to enhance readability and visual impact

- Publishing public-facing story maps to share interactive spatial analyses with a wide audience

## Tools & Data

- ArcGIS Online & ArcGIS StoryMaps: Web maps, dashboards, 3D Scene Viewer, Instant Apps, Embed functionality

- Data Sources: California Recent Earthquakes, Earthquake Faults and Folds in the USA, Population Density (Esri Demographics 2023), USGS Earthquake Hazards Program, ArcGIS Living Atlas

- Additional Resources: Earthquake preparedness imagery and links to authoritative resources (Earthquake Authority, ShakeOut.org)

- Data Management: ArcGIS Online organization account for structured map and app management, secure sharing, and public publication

## Key Insights

Developed a dashboard showing recent earthquake events, severity, and fault line locations across California, with dynamic indicators updating by map extent
Built a 3D web scene visualizing population density relative to fault lines in Los Angeles, San Diego, San Jose, and San Francisco
Embedded the dashboard and 3D web app into a StoryMap with narrative text, headings, images, and external resources for earthquake preparedness
Enhanced public accessibility by using interactive elements, slide navigation, and a visually cohesive dark-theme design
Provided Californians with actionable spatial information on earthquake hazards, emphasizing the high risk of magnitude 6.7+ events within the next 30 years
Demonstrated the ability to integrate multiple GIS applications into a cohesive, public-facing communication tool that supports awareness and preparedness efforts
